## What each tier carries

Amounts are written down in exactly one place — the [sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/Linko91)
— so they can change without a commit here.

| Tier | Where you appear |
| --- | --- |
| **Supporter** | Your name in this file and on the [docs sponsor page](https://quello-docs.vercel.app/sponsor). |
| **Backer** | Your name and a link, in both, and in the README. |
| **Partner** | Your logo and a link in all of the above, plus the docs homepage. |

A one-time sponsorship carries the same thanks and no listing: it is a tip, not a placement.

Sponsoring buys no priority and no private support — the issue tracker stays first-come. It buys the
time to keep that matrix honest.

## Other ways to help

Sponsorship is not the only currency, and at `0.1.x` it is not the most useful one:

- **Report what broke.** A pick that resolves to the wrong component, on a framework version nobody
  has tried, is worth more than a month of sponsorship.
- **Add a row to the matrix.** Rails, Laravel, Ember, Qwik — the CLI route should already work, and
  a confirmation from a real project turns a `○` into a `✅`.
- **Tell someone.** A line in an awesome-list, or a mention where your team argues about tooling,
  reaches people no launch day does.
